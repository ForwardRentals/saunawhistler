Whistler Saunas: Everything You Need to Know

A practical guide to sauna culture in Whistler, BC — where to find one, what it costs, and how a mobile sauna rental actually works after a day on the mountain.

Search "sauna Whistler" and most results are hotel spa amenities — nice, but shared, scheduled, and usually electric. There's a different option: a genuine wood-fired barrel sauna that gets delivered directly to where you're staying.

Are there public saunas in Whistler?

Most hotels and lodges in Whistler have a shared sauna as a spa amenity, but they're typically small, electric, and on a fixed schedule. For a private, wood-fired experience, Mamquam Sauna delivers a hand-crafted barrel sauna and cold plunge tub to your location for a full 24-hour booking.

What makes this sauna different from a resort spa sauna?

It's wood-fired, not electric, reaching up to 90°C with the same barrel sauna originally built on the banks of the Mamquam River in Squamish. It's also private — your 24-hour window is yours alone, with no shared schedule or strangers.

How is the delivery location arranged?

There's no fixed spot Mamquam Sauna sets up at, and delivery can't happen on public land — the sauna is only ever delivered to private property, approved in advance directly with the team. You don't just pick a location and book; reach out with where you'd like it set up (your own property, or a rental you're staying at with the owner's OK) and Glenn will confirm it works before your session is locked in.

How much does a Whistler sauna rental cost?

A full 24-hour session runs $325 flat. That includes the wood-fired barrel sauna, a cold plunge tub, all the firewood you'll need, towels, and a startup guide.

Is it good for après-ski or post-trail recovery?

That's exactly what it's built for — genuine heat-cold contrast therapy after a day on the slopes or trails, set up wherever you're staying in Whistler.

Can I book the same sauna in Squamish or Pemberton?

Yes. The same mobile sauna serves the whole Sea to Sky corridor — Whistler, Squamish, and Pemberton — so it works whether you're based on the mountain or down the highway.

How do I book?

Head to mamquamsauna.com and reserve a date directly, or send an inquiry through the mobile sauna rental page on this site if you have questions first.
