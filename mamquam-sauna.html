The Legend

From riverbank secret to a proper booking

Deep in the wild corridors of Squamish, someone built something extraordinary — a hand-crafted sauna hidden along the banks of the Mamquam River. No signs. No bookings. Just steam, silence, and the sound of the river rushing by.

For a time, it was a local secret. A place where those in the know could find warmth, community, and the kind of reset that only nature — and real heat — can provide. Then it was sold off, and the legend faded into the trees.

That's where Shuwn and Glenn came in. They purchased the original sauna, preserved its soul, and rebuilt it as Mamquam Sauna, now bookable across Whistler, Squamish, and Pemberton.

"Heat. Cold. Repeat. Remember what it feels like to be alive."
