The Legend

Born on the Mamquam River

Deep in the wild corridors of Squamish, someone built something extraordinary — a hand-crafted sauna hidden along the banks of the Mamquam River. No signs, no bookings, just steam, silence, and the sound of the river rushing by.

FAQ

Whistler sauna questions, answered

Is there a mobile sauna rental in Whistler?

Yes — Mamquam Sauna delivers a wood-fired barrel sauna and cold plunge tub to a private location in Whistler for a 24-hour booking window.

What does a Whistler sauna rental include?

A full 24-hour private rental includes the wood-fired barrel sauna, a cold plunge tub, all the firewood you need, towels, and a startup guide. It runs $325 per session.

Is it a good après-ski option?

It's built for exactly that — genuine wood-fired heat up to 90°C and a cold plunge, set up at your accommodation after a day on the mountain.

Can I book the same sauna in Squamish or Pemberton?

Yes — the same mobile sauna rental serves the whole Sea to Sky corridor: Whistler, Squamish, and Pemberton.

Can I have it delivered anywhere I want?

No — there's no fixed spot Mamquam Sauna sets up at, and delivery can't happen on public property. The location has to be private property, and it's approved directly with the team before your session is confirmed. Reach out with where you'd like it set up and they'll let you know if it works.
