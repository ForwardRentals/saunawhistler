Sauna in Pemberton: A Private Wood-Fired Sauna, Delivered

Pemberton has big valley views, farms and mountains, and not many places to sauna. Here's how to get a wood-fired barrel sauna delivered.

Pemberton is the quieter end of the Sea to Sky: a wide farming valley under Mount Currie, half an hour or so past Whistler. It's a beautiful place for a sauna, and the easiest way to get one is to have it delivered.

A sauna that comes to you

Mamquam Sauna is a hand-built, wood-fired barrel sauna that travels the whole corridor: Squamish, Whistler and Pemberton. Glenn delivers and sets up the sauna and a cold plunge tub at your spot, and you get it privately for a full 24 hours.

  • Wood-fired, reaching up to about 90°C.
  • Fits about 8 people comfortably.
  • Cold plunge tub and firewood included.
  • $325 for 24 hours, or $1,500 for a week.

Where it works in Pemberton

The sauna needs a reasonably level spot it can be delivered to, and permission from whoever owns the land. Places it suits:

  • Your own home or backyard in Pemberton, Mount Currie or up the valley.
  • A cabin or vacation rental, with your host's OK.
  • A farm stay or acreage with room to spread out.
  • A celebration, like a wedding weekend, birthday or family reunion at a private property.

There's no fixed spot it sets up at. Describe where you'd like it in your booking request and Glenn will confirm the details before your booking is locked in.

Why Pemberton is perfect for a sauna

Clear valley nights, cold winters and hot summers, and room to breathe. In winter, the valley gets real cold, which makes for brilliant contrast between the sauna and the snow. In summer, a sauna session as the heat of the day fades is hard to beat. Wherever you are, the mountains are the backdrop.

Pair it with a Pemberton day

  • A hike: there are great trails all around the valley, from short lake walks to big alpine days.
  • A farm visit: Pemberton is known for its farms. Stock up and turn the sauna night into a feast.
  • A paddle: lakes near Pemberton are lovely on a calm day.
  • A ride: Pemberton has excellent mountain biking, and the sauna is the reward at the end.

How booking works

Hit Book Now, pick your dates and send a request. Nothing is charged to send it, and you can include questions. Your dates are held for 48 hours while Glenn reviews the request. Once it's approved you'll get an email link to pay a $100 deposit, which confirms the booking. The balance is sent as a payment link three days before your session.

A few safety basics

  • Drink water before, during and after.
  • Skip the alcohol until you're done.
  • If you're pregnant or have a heart condition or blood pressure issues, check with your doctor before using a sauna or cold plunge.
  • Keep the fire contained, follow any local fire restrictions, and keep the area around the stove clear.

Questions

Is there a sauna in Pemberton?

Pemberton doesn't have many public sauna options. Mamquam Sauna delivers a private wood-fired barrel sauna and cold plunge to your location in Pemberton for a 24-hour session.

How much does a sauna rental in Pemberton cost?

A 24-hour session is $325, which includes the wood-fired barrel sauna, cold plunge tub and firewood. A full week is $1,500.

Can you deliver a sauna to a farm stay or Airbnb in Pemberton?

Yes, with the property owner's permission. Describe the setup spot in your booking request and Glenn will confirm it works before the booking is locked in.
